# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Working assumption pending optional user steering: static HTML/CSS and JavaScript ES modules, checked with TypeScript. No runtime dependencies. The 40,000-byte source ceiling favors a small, inspectable implementation.

## Users
Individuals recording places visited and places they hope to visit, as explicitly described in brief.txt.

## Product Purpose
A personal digital passport whose colored stamps hold dates and memories. Success means adding, revisiting, correcting and preserving these records without an account.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Place, visit date, five ink colors, short memory, visited/dream statuses, counts, CSS-only circular stamps on rectangular pages, localStorage and confirmed deletion. Under 40 KB raw source. No backend/login. Rubric and detailed audit: REQUIREMENTS.md.

## Brand Commitments
Stamp Rally; passport-page grid and worn, gently rotated circular ink stamps are brief-pinned.

## Evidence on Hand
prompt.md and brief.txt. No supplied images or personal travel data.

## Product Principles
Keep entry quick. Make status explicit. Preserve memories on failure. Make corrections reversible. Do not invent collected stamps.
